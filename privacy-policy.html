Table Snatcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

What data we collect

Table Snatcher does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All table extraction and data processing happens entirely within your browser.

Local storage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) solely to track your daily export count and Pro license status. This data never leaves your device and is automatically cleaned up after 7 days.

Permissions explained

activeTab — Allows the extension to read the current page when you click the extension icon, so it can find and extract tables. It cannot access any page you haven't explicitly activated the extension on.

scripting — Required to inject the table detection script into the active tab when you click the extension icon.

storage — Used to save your daily usage count and Pro status locally on your device.

downloads — Used to save exported CSV, JSON, or ZIP files to your Downloads folder when you click a download button.

Third-party services

If you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed by ExtensionPay (powered by Stripe). Table Snatcher itself never sees or stores your payment details. Pro is a one-time purchase — no recurring billing. Please refer to Stripe's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

Analytics and tracking

Table Snatcher contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or any form of data collection. We don't know who you are, what sites you visit, or what tables you extract.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties. There is no data to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
